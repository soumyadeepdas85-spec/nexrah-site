Hero headline photos
--------------------
Drop two images in this folder and they appear in the hero automatically:
  chip-1.jpg   first image, after "Brands Built". PORTRAIT 3:4 (for example 600 x 800 px).
  chip-2.jpg   second image, after "What's Next". CIRCLE: use a SQUARE image (for example 600 x 600 px);
               it is cropped to a circle automatically.
.png and .webp also work. Other shapes are cropped to fit.
